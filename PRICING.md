## 🎯 Which License is Right for You?

### Choose **Open Source** if:
- You're a startup with limited budget
- You have technical expertise for self-support
- You want to test the foundation before committing
- You're building an internal tool or side project
- You're an educational institution or open-source project

### Choose **Enterprise** if:
- You need guaranteed support and SLAs
- You're processing sensitive customer data
- You require compliance certifications (HIPAA, SOC2, etc.)
- You want priority access to new features
- You need help with architecture and deployment
- You're a growing SaaS company ready to scale

### Choose **White Label** if:
- You're building a product to sell to customers
- You're an agency delivering solutions to clients
- You want to rebrand the foundation as your own
- You need unlimited client deployments
- You require deep customization and integration
- You want to influence the product roadmap

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I try Enterprise features before purchasing?
Yes! We offer a **14-day free trial** of the Enterprise license. No credit card required.

### Can I upgrade from Open Source to Enterprise later?
Absolutely! Upgrading is seamless. Your existing code and data remain unchanged.

### What happens if I cancel my Enterprise license?
You can continue using the Open Source version. Premium features will be disabled, but your data remains intact.

### Can I mix license types (e.g., Enterprise for production, Open Source for development)?
Yes! This is a common setup. Enterprise license covers production environments only.

### Do you offer custom licensing arrangements?
Yes, for unique situations we can create custom agreements. Contact our sales team.

### Is there a limit on the number of users/organizations?
No! All licenses support unlimited users and organizations. Pricing is based on support level, not usage.

### Can I get a refund?
Enterprise and White Label licenses include a 90-day pro-rated refund policy.

### What if I need more custom development hours?
Additional hours can be purchased in 40-hour blocks at $10,000/block for Enterprise customers.

### Do you offer discounts for long-term contracts?
Yes! Multi-year commitments receive 10-15% additional discounts.

### Can non-technical teams use this?
The foundation is developer-focused. However, Enterprise customers get training and onboarding to help non-technical stakeholders understand the platform.
